Black Folder

Privacy Policy for the Black Folder Android application.

Black Folder is designed as a local-first document wallet. Your documents are stored on your device and protected using Android security mechanisms. Some optional services, including advertising and Google Play purchases, necessarily involve third parties.

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Black Folder handles information when you use the Android application.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Black Folder is designed to keep your personal document collection on your device rather than uploading your documents to a Black Folder cloud account.

Depending on the features you use, information stored locally can include:

  • Documents, scanned pages, PDFs and images that you import or capture.
  • Document titles, notes, categories, document numbers and other metadata you enter.
  • Family-member and property information you choose to enter.
  • Application preferences, security settings and local application state.
  • Trash and recovery information used to support document recovery.

The application uses app-private storage and local encryption mechanisms for protected document content. We do not operate a Black Folder cloud service for storing your document collection.

2. Camera and Imported Files

Black Folder may request camera access so you can scan documents. Camera data is used for the scanning feature and is not collected by Black Folder as a remote account record.

You may also import files or images through Android's file and sharing interfaces. Files you choose to import become part of your local Black Folder document collection.

3. Security and Biometrics

Black Folder can use Android biometric authentication and application security features to protect access to your local document collection. Authentication information such as your biometric template remains managed by Android and is not provided to Black Folder.

Black Folder may use encryption and Android Keystore-backed keys to protect stored document content.

Do not uninstall the application, clear its data, or remove security credentials without first ensuring that you have an appropriate backup or export of anything you need to retain.

4. Advertising

Black Folder may display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Advertising providers may process device and advertising-related information as described in their own policies.

Where required, Black Folder uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent and privacy choices.

Third-party advertising services may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information, subject to the provider's configuration and applicable law.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information .

5. Google Play Purchases

Black Folder may offer paid premium features through Google Play Billing. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play.

Black Folder may receive purchase information necessary to determine whether premium access has been purchased and to provide the corresponding features.

Black Folder does not receive or store your full payment-card number through the application. Payment processing is handled by Google.

See Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

6. Network Connections

The core document-management functionality is designed to work locally. Network access may nevertheless occur for third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads, Google consent services, Google Play Billing, and purchase-verification infrastructure when configured by the application.

Black Folder does not intentionally upload your stored document collection to a Black Folder-operated cloud server.

7. Notifications

If notification permission is granted, Black Folder may use Android notifications for application-related reminders or status information. You can control notification permission through Android system settings.

8. Data Sharing

Black Folder does not sell your personal documents or personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when you use their services through Black Folder, including Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent-management services. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies.

You can intentionally share or print documents using Android's sharing and printing features. Once you send a document to another application or service, that recipient's privacy practices apply.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored documents and related information remain on your device until you delete them, clear the application's data, uninstall the application, or otherwise remove the relevant files.

Black Folder does not maintain a central cloud copy of your document collection that we can delete on your behalf.

Third-party providers may retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

10. Children's Privacy

Black Folder is not specifically directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children as a separate online account service.

11. Your Choices

  • You can choose what documents and personal information you enter into Black Folder.
  • You can deny or revoke camera permission through Android settings.
  • You can control notification permission through Android settings.
  • You can manage available advertising consent and privacy choices inside the application.
  • You can delete locally stored documents or application data using the application's controls and Android system settings.

12. Security Disclaimer

We take reasonable measures to protect locally stored information, but no software or device can guarantee absolute security.

Keep your Android device protected with a strong device lock and maintain safe copies of important documents when appropriate.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's features, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official support/contact channel provided with the Black Folder application or its Google Play listing.

Please do not post sensitive personal documents or private information in a public issue or repository discussion.
